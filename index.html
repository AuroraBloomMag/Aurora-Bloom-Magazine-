Our story

A magazine made
with love

Aurora Bloom was born from a simple belief — that every woman deserves a space that celebrates who she is, in full. A space where modest fashion is elevated, where books are savoured, where recipes carry heritage, and where faith is lived beautifully. Founded by a Muslim woman and rooted in Islamic values, Aurora Bloom warmly welcomes all women to its pages.

Published bi-monthly, each issue is crafted with care and intention. Whether you're here for outfit inspiration, a good read, a new recipe, or a quiet reflection — you belong here.

Creative Bloom

Share your light with us

Welcome to Aurora Bloom's Creative Bloom — a cozy, inspiring space where your creativity is celebrated. Whether you write from the heart, paint with purpose, or capture beauty through your lens, we'd love to showcase your work!

✨ What you can submit

  • Poetry (up to 40 lines)
  • Short stories (max 2,000 words)
  • Personal reflections or journal-style pieces (400–800 words)
  • Original photography or digital artwork
  • Islamic calligraphy or visual art inspired by modesty, spirituality, or sisterhood

🌷 What we're looking for

  • Original work — if previously published, please let us know where
  • Respectful, uplifting, and reflective work

🚫 What to avoid

  • Content with explicit, inappropriate, or insensitive themes
  • Plagiarised or AI-generated submissions
